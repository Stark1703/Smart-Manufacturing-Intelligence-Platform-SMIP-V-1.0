# Gold Layer

## Purpose

The Gold Layer provides business-ready datasets optimized for reporting, dashboarding, and KPI analysis.

It aggregates manufacturing data into analytical summaries for decision-makers.

---

## Gold Tables

### Production Summary

Production KPIs by product and date.

### Quality Summary

Quality metrics including pass/fail rates.

### OEE Summary

Availability, Performance, Quality, and Overall Equipment Effectiveness.

### Traceability Summary

End-to-end product genealogy.

### Executive Summary

High-level manufacturing KPIs.
