# Traceability Dashboard

## Overview

The Traceability Dashboard provides complete genealogy for every manufactured product.

Users can trace products from work order creation through production, quality inspection, material usage, and packaging.

## Business Objective

Enable complete manufacturing traceability.

---

## Traceability Information

- Serial Number
- Work Order
- Product
- Machine
- Operator
- Tool
- Material
- Batch Number
- Supplier
- Test Results
- Packaging

---

## Visualizations

- Product Genealogy
- Manufacturing Timeline
- Material Traceability
- Quality Status
- Force Analysis

---

## Target Users

- Manufacturing Engineer
- Quality Engineer
- Customer Support
- Production Manager

## Business Value

Supports root cause analysis, quality investigations, and product genealogy.
