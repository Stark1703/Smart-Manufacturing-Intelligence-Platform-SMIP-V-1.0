# Manufacturing KPIs

## Overview

SMIP calculates key manufacturing performance indicators (KPIs) from the Gold Layer datasets.

These KPIs provide standardized metrics for monitoring production performance and operational excellence.

## Production KPIs

- Units Produced
- Completed Work Orders
- Average Order Quantity
- Production Trend

---

## Quality KPIs

- Tests Performed
- Passed Tests
- Failed Tests
- Pass Rate
- Fail Rate

---

## OEE KPIs

- Availability
- Performance
- Quality
- Overall Equipment Effectiveness

---

## Traceability KPIs

- Serial Numbers
- Material Scans
- Packaging Records
- Product Genealogy

---

## Executive KPIs

- Plant OEE
- Pass Rate
- Units Produced
- Work Orders Completed

---

## Business Benefits

These KPIs support:

- Executive reporting
- Production planning
- Process optimization
- Continuous improvement
- Manufacturing analytics
