# Factory Digital Twin

## Overview

The Factory Digital Twin represents a virtual model of the manufacturing facility used by SMIP to simulate realistic production operations.

The Digital Twin defines the hierarchical relationships between production assets and provides the foundation for manufacturing simulations.

---

## Factory Hierarchy

The Digital Twin consists of:

- Factory
- Production Halls
- Production Lines
- Stations
- Machines
- Operators
- Products
- Tools
- Operations
- Press Programs
- Test Programs

---

## Purpose

The Digital Twin enables:

- Production Planning
- Manufacturing Simulation
- Resource Allocation
- Product Traceability
- Performance Analytics

---

## Manufacturing Assets

### Factory

Represents the manufacturing facility.

### Production Hall

Logical grouping of production lines.

### Production Line

Manufacturing line responsible for product assembly.

### Station

Individual manufacturing workstation.

### Machine

Physical manufacturing equipment.

### Operator

Manufacturing personnel assigned to machines.

### Product

Manufactured high-voltage electrical equipment.

---

## Benefits

The Digital Twin enables realistic simulation while maintaining consistency across all generated manufacturing datasets.
