# Press Fitting Dashboard

## Overview

The Press Fitting Dashboard analyzes machine performance during press fitting operations.

It provides detailed visibility into production efficiency and Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE).

---

## Business Objective

Monitor manufacturing equipment performance.

---

## KPIs

- Availability
- Performance
- Quality
- Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)
- Cycle Time
- Average Force

---

## Visualizations

- OEE Trend
- OEE by Machine
- Cycle Time
- Force Analysis
- Machine Ranking
- Production by Shift

---

## Target Users

- Manufacturing Engineer
- Process Engineer
- Maintenance Engineer

---

## Business Value

Supports process optimization and equipment performance analysis.
