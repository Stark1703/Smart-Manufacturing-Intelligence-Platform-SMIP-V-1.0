# Production Dashboard

## Overview

The Production Dashboard focuses on manufacturing throughput and production planning.

It enables supervisors to evaluate production performance across products, shifts, and manufacturing lines.

---

## Business Objective

Monitor production volume and identify throughput trends.

---

## KPIs

- Units Produced
- Executions
- Completed Work Orders
- Average Order Quantity

---

## Visualizations

- Daily Production Trend
- Product Mix
- Shift Distribution
- Production by Product
- Production Calendar

---

## Target Users

- Production Manager
- Manufacturing Engineer
- Production Planner

---

## Business Value

Supports production planning, scheduling, and resource allocation.
