# Quality Dashboard

## Overview

The Quality Dashboard monitors manufacturing quality performance and inspection results.

It provides visibility into product quality across manufacturing operations.

## Business Objective

Measure production quality and identify failure trends.

---

## KPIs

- Tests Performed
- Passed Tests
- Failed Tests
- Pass Rate
- Fail Rate

---

## Visualizations

- Pass vs Fail
- Pass Rate Trend
- Test Results by Product
- Shift Quality
- Quality Calendar

---

## Target Users

- Quality Manager
- Quality Engineer
- Manufacturing Engineer

---

## Business Value

Supports continuous improvement initiatives and quality monitoring.
