# Executive Dashboard

## Overview

The Executive Dashboard provides a high-level overview of manufacturing performance.

It is designed for plant managers and operations leadership who require quick visibility into key manufacturing KPIs.

---

## Business Objective

Monitor overall factory performance using executive-level metrics.

---

## KPIs

- Units Produced
- Work Orders Completed
- Pass Rate
- Plant OEE

---

## Visualizations

- KPI Cards
- Production Trend
- Product Mix
- Shift Performance
- OEE Status
- Production Calendar

---

## Target Users

- Plant Manager
- Operations Director
- Production Manager

---

## Business Value

Provides immediate visibility into factory performance and supports operational decision-making.
